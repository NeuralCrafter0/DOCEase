# DOCEase - Clinical Roadmap & Idea Repository

## ?? 1. Core Time-Saving Features (Highest Priority)
*   **Smart Prescription Builder:** 
    *   Medicine autocomplete.
    *   Dosage templates (OD / BD / TDS).
    *   Favorite medicines list.
    *   Quick prescription editing.
*   **Quick Visit Mode:** 
    *   Direct "New Visit" action from patient list.
    *   Add diagnosis + prescription in seconds with minimal typing.
*   **Repeat Previous Prescription:** 
    *   "Repeat Last Visit" button to auto-fill previous data.
*   **Smart Patient Search:** 
    *   Refined partial text matching for names and phone numbers.

## ?? 2. Patient Management Improvements
*   **Timeline Enhancements:** Color-coded markers for Initial, Follow-up, and Routine visits.
*   **Vitals Section:** Optional fields for BP, Temp, Pulse, Weight, and Sugar.
*   **Lab Report Attachment:** Support for PDFs and high-res document scans with comparison view.

## ? 3. Workflow Automation
*   **Follow-Up Reminders:** 7-day/custom reminders with system notifications and WhatsApp integration.
*   **Quick Disease Templates:** One-tap auto-fill for Fever, Diabetes, Hypertension, etc.

## ?? 4. Trust & UX
*   **Auto Backup:** Seamless background cloud synchronization.
*   **Data Export:** Allow doctors to export records to standard formats.
*   **Global Search:** Always-on search bar on the dashboard.
*   **Pagination:** Instant loading for thousands of patient records.
